How to read it

<30: weak participation. >70: broad participation. Extremes can persist and are not automatic reversal signals.

Volume balance: positive means abnormal activity is more concentrated in rising stocks; negative means more in falling stocks.

Public display: aggregate-only output. Stock-level vendor data and credentials remain private.
