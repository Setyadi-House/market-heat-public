Smoothing

Raw observations are never fabricated. Missing source sessions remain gaps.

Coverage

Valid indicator observations / warmed target stocks. A missing whole-market source date is shown as a gap, not as 0%.

Coverage below 95% means some eligible stocks lack a valid indicator observation. N/A means no usable stock cross-section existed for that date.

How to read it

Breadth: <30 = weak participation, 30–70 = mixed, >70 = broad. Extremes can persist and are not automatic reversal signals.

Volume Activity: 100 = normal relative volume. 130 means aggregate activity is roughly 30% above normal after per-stock normalisation and capping.

Directional Balance: positive = abnormal volume is concentrated more in rising stocks; negative = more in falling stocks; 0 = balanced. This is not money flow.

High-volume breadth: percentage of valid stocks trading at least 1.5× their own prior-20-observed-session average volume.
